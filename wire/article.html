Eagle Wire is a fictional wire service created for USAI | The American Ideal, a Roblox roleplay community. It is not a real news organization, is not affiliated with any real broadcaster, newspaper or wire service, and does not report on real people or real events. Every byline is a character name and every story is roleplay.
