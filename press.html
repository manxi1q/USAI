How it works

Written by members, published by editors

Each newsroom runs on the same publishing system: reporters draft, an editor reviews, and the piece goes live with a byline and a dateline. Corrections are issued publicly, the same as anywhere that takes itself seriously.

  • Every article covers in-universe events only.
  • Bylines are character names, never real identities.
  • Slant is allowed. Fabricating quotes from other members is not.
  • Editors answer to the community rules before their editorial line.
