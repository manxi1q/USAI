Your first day

Four steps in

No application, no interview, no waiting list. Most newcomers are in a session within a day.

  • First

    Join the Discord

    It is the front door. Read the primer channel, then introduce yourself — a name, and what you would like to try.

  • Second

    Join the Roblox community

    Membership is the roster. It is what gets you into the Capitol when the floor opens.

  • Third

    Pick a lane

    A party, a state, a newsroom, a clerkship, a campaign staff. You can change your mind later; almost everyone does.

  • Fourth

    Show up to a session

    Watch one from the gallery before you speak, if you would rather. Then write something and put it on the calendar.

What you can be

Roles for newcomers

Legislator

Run for a seat, write directives, work a committee. The most direct route into the argument.

Reporter

File for Eagle Wire. You learn the country twice as fast when you have to explain it to someone else.

Campaign staff

Message, strategy, endorsements and turnout for someone else's race. Underrated and always needed.

Clerk or counsel

Work the courts: research, briefs and oral argument. For people who like being right in writing.

Cabinet staff

Run a department, file its reports, and defend its record when the press comes asking.

Archivist

Keep the wiki. Every election and ruling needs someone who writes it down properly.

Community standards

Non-toxic, and we mean it

USAI was founded on the idea that you can argue hard about a fictional country and still be decent to the person on the other side of the screen. The rules below are not decorative — moderation enforces them.

  • In character is in character. Never carry a dispute back to the person.
  • No harassment, slurs, or targeting anyone over who they are.
  • No real-world political campaigning, fundraising or advocacy. This is a simulation of a fictional country.
  • Keep everything appropriate for the Roblox audience: no explicit, graphic or mature content anywhere in the community.
  • Never share personal information — yours or anyone else's — in character or out.
  • No hate symbols or extremist content, in roleplay or out of it.
  • Roblox and Discord rules come first. Where ours are stricter, ours apply.

A note for parents

What this community is

USAI is a text-and-voice roleplay about a fictional country modelled loosely on American civics. Members write legislation, hold debates, run mock elections and publish fictional news.

There is no real-world political affiliation, no fundraising, and no real news reporting. Moderators are active on both Discord and Roblox, and all members are held to the Roblox Community Standards.
