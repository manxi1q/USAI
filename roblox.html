The community

USAI on Roblox

The Roblox community is the country's roster. Ranks track the offices you hold in character, and membership is what gets you into the room when a session opens.

Joining is instant and free. Bring your Discord alongside it — that is where scheduling, debate prep and everything out of character happens.

Before you play

House rules inside the experience

  • Stay in character in session; take disputes to Discord.
  • No trolling, spawn-camping or interrupting proceedings.
  • Follow the Roblox Community Standards — they outrank ours.
  • Never share personal information, yours or anyone else's.

Where the country meets

Each venue hosts a different part of the simulation. Sessions are announced on Discord ahead of time — anyone may attend the public ones.

The Capitol

Chambers, committee rooms and a public gallery. Floor debate and recorded votes happen here on session nights.
